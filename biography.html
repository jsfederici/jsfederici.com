Jacopo Sam Federici è un producer, songwriter e arrangiatore nato a Latina e basato a Milano. Il suo lavoro attraversa scrittura, produzione, recording e orchestrazione. Il suo approccio unisce visione musicale, cura della sessione e rapidità operativa.

Jacopo Sam Federici is a producer, songwriter and arranger born in Latina, based in Milan. His work moves through writing, production, recording and orchestration. His approach brings together musical vision, attentiveness in session and operational precision.

La sua carriera è iniziata sotto la guida di Nicolò Fragile, che lo ha ingaggiato come assistente a vent'anni — un rapporto di lavoro diventato nel tempo una collaborazione stretta e continuativa.

His career began under the mentorship of Nicolò Fragile, who brought him on as an assistant at twenty — a working relationship that has grown into a close, ongoing collaboration.

Ha collaborato con artisti come Eros Ramazzotti, Renato Zero, Ligabue, Mika, Ornella Vanoni, Ron, Paola & Chiara, Tazenda e molti altri. Collabora con la Budapest Art Orchestra come trascrittore e coordinatore orchestrale.

He has collaborated with artists including Eros Ramazzotti, Renato Zero, Ligabue, Mika, Ornella Vanoni, Ron, Paola & Chiara, Tazenda and many others. He works with the Budapest Art Orchestra as a transcriber and orchestral coordinator.

Dal 2023 lavora come composer e producer su brief per Hallwood Media, realtà internazionale basata a Los Angeles focalizzata su songwriter e producer.

Since 2023 he has been composing and producing on brief for Hallwood Media, an international company based in Los Angeles focused on songwriters and producers.

Gran parte delle sue produzioni recenti è firmata FET BASTARDs, il duo formato con Iacopo Pinna, producer multiplatino basato a Los Angeles. Insieme hanno lavorato a progetti arrivati in Europa e Nord America, con ingegneri come Collin Dupuis, Dave Way, Gavin Lurssen e Randy Merrill e contributi in sessione di Mike Portnoy e Sterling Laws.

Most of his recent productions are credited under FET BASTARDs, the duo he formed with Iacopo Pinna, a multi-platinum producer based in Los Angeles. Together they have worked on projects reaching Europe and North America, with engineers including Collin Dupuis, Dave Way, Gavin Lurssen and Randy Merrill, and session contributions from Mike Portnoy and Sterling Laws.
